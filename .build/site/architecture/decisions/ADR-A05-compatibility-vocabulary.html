ADR A-05: Compatibility operation vocabulary

Status: Published, 2026-10-19. Accepted 2026-09-17. Described in: Eligibility README

Context

Eligibility conditions need a small, stable operation vocabulary to express how multiple requirements combine. Free-text or ad hoc operation naming prevents conformance checks from being portable.

Decision

Eligibility declares a closed-by-default mechanism vocabulary for compatibility operations with three named operations:

  • elg:AllRequired
  • elg:AnySufficient
  • elg:DimensionConsistent

These are individuals of elg:CompatibilityOperation and are used by elg:compatibilityOperation.

Consequences

  • Every condition states combination semantics in a machine-checkable way.
  • Implementations can support profile-level checks without relying on implementation-specific labels.
  • Downstream extensions may add operations, but baseline conformance depends on these three names.

Addendum, 2026-10-19: composition operations, on profiles only

Agreed by the maintainer. The vocabulary says how an admission profile combines its conditions’ outcomes, so it is renamed elg:CompositionOperation, used by elg:compositionOperation, and only an admission profile declares one. On a condition that is not a profile it had no effect, and a shape now reports it there. elg:DimensionConsistent is removed, with the operational profile elg:E6 that tested it. It was meant to check that conditions on different dimensions are compatible before they are combined, which is a precondition rather than a way of composing outcomes, and no compiler could evaluate it. The baseline is elg:AllRequired and elg:AnySufficient. A downstream ontology may still add an operation, and the compilers refuse one they cannot evaluate.

Provenance

Accepted when Eligibility was first specified, 2026-09-17. The 2026-10-19 addendum answers question OD6-Q7 of the improved-ontology-documentation plan.
