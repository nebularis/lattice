3. Design-Time Authoring

The moving parts behind authoring a contract or a template: drafting wording, mapping external schemes, defining eligibility conditions, and everything that checks the result before it is ever evaluated against a real case. See docs/architecture/ux-design.md for the two products’ own UX specification and docs/architecture/surface-workflow.md for the revision lifecycle in full.
