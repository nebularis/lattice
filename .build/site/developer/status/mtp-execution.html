Summary

The plan document mtp-implementation-plan.md outlined a complete, phased roadmap for the MORK Teaching Pack (MTP) — a machine-generated curriculum for teaching language models valid MCN without memorizing the full 45k-token ontology. Every slice in that plan has been authored and validated.
