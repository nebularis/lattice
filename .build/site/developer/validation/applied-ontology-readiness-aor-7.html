Validation Pack: AOR-7, SHACL, SWRL and diagnostics for concept plans

Unit: applied-ontology-readiness Plan section: Phase B Decisions: ADR-A89 items 3, 4 and 6, ADR-A24, ADR-A28

Invariant

SHACL agrees with the SPARQL reference on every concept-plan case. SWRL derives only what SPARQL decides as Permitted or Denied, each from a positive fact, and never Undetermined. Every Undetermined row carries a declared diagnostic, and no other row carries one.

SWRL rules are applied by apply_rules, which evaluates each rule body as a conjunctive query. That is exact for rules with class and individual-property atoms only, and it refuses any other atom.

Known limit

SWRL cannot tell one candidate from several. A question with two candidates derives an outcome per candidate. AOR7-07 and AOR7-08 use one candidate per question. ADR-A89’s consequences record this.
