Validation Pack: FMH-H2.6, the expected compiled profile and the paper check

Unit: formal-methods-track-h, slice H2.6, planned in plan §4.4 and the typed IR sketch §7.2, after H2.5. Examples first (lattice-lifecycle): this slice stops here for your review, and for your commit of the expected profiles, before H2.7 is briefed against them. For the maintainer: the model and the proposed names (below), the paper check, and eight questions at the end. The structure is fixed, so the review is about names and about the questions. For countersignature (non-weakening rule): none. No test was removed, skipped or loosened. The only change to code that earlier slices tested is additive: an access now carries a mode, and the audit builders tag their result variables with kinds.

Invariant

The compiled profile of an example, once H2.7 emits it, says for every generated operation what a caller in any language, a relational realisation and a model checker need, and says it without SPARQL text and without a blank node. The files that say it are held to the builders (so they cannot drift from the code that produces the queries) and to today’s compile (so they cannot drift from the profile). Where the files differ from the builders on purpose, the difference is listed with its reason and the slice that closes it.

What changed

  • persistence.contract (production code, plan step 1). contract_of(operation) derives the kind of an operation, its builder version, its guard status, its parameters, the logical resources it accesses with the mode and key of each access, the columns of an audit, and its payload and request-digest contracts. Three tables are kept there and nowhere else: OPERATION_KINDS, resource_of (a graph role, and for the keys graph the predicate, to a logical resource) and BUILDER_VERSIONS. HAND_DECLARED holds the contract of cas-replace-value-guard, which has no builder (TD-43).
  • Access carries a mode (persistence.ir.AccessMode), set by the checker: an absence read inside NOT EXISTS, a whole-aggregate read through a path or of every triple of a root’s graph, a scan, a point read, an insert, a delete. The audit builders tag their result variables with kinds.
  • The expected profiles, nine files and one more, in ontology/persistence/test/expected-profiles/: the eight examples of plan H2.6-Q2 and the variant baseline-single-class.caller-named (guards off, the caller names each graph), and kinds.expected.ttl, which declares the closed enumerations the others name. They are written by tests/expected_profiles.py, and the ontology README (§13) and the persistence README describe them.
  • Tests (test_expected_profiles.py, test_builder_versions.py, 114 cases): parse, no blank node and no SPARQL text, the base profile, the agreement with the builders in three outcomes, the dataset-guard claim, the parameter names, minted IRIs, the enumerations, and the golden check on builder versions.
  • Documents. This pack, the status record, the plan, the index. The Validation Pack of H2.5 is unchanged.

Every declaration node has an IRI, <target with # as />/compiled/<operation name>#<part>, for example https://example.org/lending/LoanApplication/compiled/cas-replace#param-root, with the parts param-<name>, access-<n>, result-<name>, payload, digest and digest-part-<n>. Mapping findings (plan step 1). Checked against all 27 builders. Only the keys graph holds two resources, told apart by predicate (MergeRecord for any predicate outside the claim’s own). The meta graph holds the version row only, and that includes the stream counter of append-event, which is the stream’s version row. ?log ranges over the log buckets and the pinned graph, and the mapping takes both as Receipt (NQ1, decided). The pinned graph is how retention is realised, and the paper check below tests that as a hypothesis.

The paper check

Against lattice-sql.md §9 (the persistence dimensions) and §4.2 (the concurrency primitives). It is on paper, since no relational compiler exists (ADR-A-FM4 decision 1). Three outcomes: logical (both realisations have the resource), realisation only (an artefact of one of them), gap (for you). The conclusions are hypotheses for you to confirm or reject, not decisions.

Per resource

Questions for the review

  1. The names. Every dal: term above is a proposal, in kinds.expected.ttl and the files. Which would you change?
  2. dal:templateId keeps its .mustache suffix in the files, so that the base-profile test stays a pure subset of compile. The builder’s identity is the operation id without it. Should H2.7 drop the suffix, with H2.9?
  3. The set of resource kinds. Event, MergeRecord and KeyQuarantine could each be folded into another (the event into the receipt, the merge record and the quarantine into the key claim). They have their own keys in both realisations, so they are kept apart. Agree?
  4. The pinned head as a realisation of Receipt (NQ1 and the paper check). Confirm, or give the pinned graph a resource of its own.
  5. The digest of append-event. The contract says the digest is taken over the operation kind, the target, the expected sequence and the sorted canonical N-Triples of the payload. Append has no payload parameter and no expected sequence, so its file shows both parts as dal:NoSource, and what the event contributes is undeclared. H2.8 builds the algorithm. Who decides the append’s input?
  6. The patch content (the gap above). Is the patch written by a separate generated operation, by the caller, or is it a resource of the receipt?
  7. The three gaps in §9 (the transaction claim, the retention mark, tombstone and ProvidedConcurrency). They are findings about lattice-sql.md, not the contract. Do you want them recorded as a review request on that note?
  8. oldValue and newValue of the value guard are declared IriOrLiteral, since the template says nothing about their type. TD-43 settles it. Fine for now?

Deliberate non-coverage

  • The vocabulary, its shapes and the compiler’s emission (H2.7). The files are not validated against any shape, and the terms are not in the ontology.
  • The composite dataset-guard variants as files. H2.6-T6 asserts what makes the omission acceptable.
  • The relational realisation, which is on paper only (ADR-A-FM4 decision 1).
  • The digest algorithm beyond its declaration (H2.8), and isolation semantics (H5).
  • A file for each combination of receipt model and concurrency profile. The nine cover each operation kind and each resource.
