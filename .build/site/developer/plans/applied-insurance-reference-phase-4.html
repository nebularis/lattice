Notes for this slice’s concepts (review of earlier designs, 2026-10-13). The value types cite the Open Exposure Data (OED) location fields they correspond to (Building to BuildingTIV, Contents to ContentsTIV, GrossProfit, Revenue and Rental to BITIV, Machinery, Stock and OtherValue to OtherTIV), and say where OED is coarser. The legal forms are generic kinds. Their notes cite ISO 20275 (Entity Legal Forms), whose codes are per jurisdiction and run to thousands, so a deployment binds an ISO 20275 edition by territory, mapped to these kinds by skos:broadMatch. The reference scheme is the unscoped fallback. Programme roles, declared as icm-voc:Claimant is: aeo-voc:NamedInsured, FirstNamedInsured, AdditionalInsured, LossPayee, LendersLossPayee, Mortgagee, LessorRole (distinct from the interest kind Lessor), Bailor and WaiverOfSubrogationBeneficiary. LendersLossPayee is narrower than LossPayee (review R10). Its note cites the ISO commercial property loss payable provisions endorsement (CP 12 18), whose lender’s loss payable clause gives the lender rights a simple loss payee lacks, and the London market’s practice of noting a lender’s interest with a loss payee clause. The waiver beneficiary’s note says the waiver itself is a contract term (Phase 5) and the role only names who benefits. The additional insured variants by ISO endorsement (CG 20 10, CG 20 37, CG 20 38) are conferred by the contract and wait for Phase 5.

Derivation of exposure units (tools/exposure_derivations.py)

exposure_units(graph, perils, run) returns the units for every location of the exposure set, asset class present there and peril in perils, as a separate graph. It never writes into the source graph. A unit’s value is the sum of the amounts of the valuations of that class’s assets at that location, when they share one currency. With several currencies and no conversion in the graph, the value is omitted and the function reports it, since converting needs a rate and a date (Quantification’s pattern). A peril applies to a location’s unit when the location has any asset (the function does not decide physical exposure, which is a model’s work).

“On demand” (sketch §5.11, route R1) means a check calls this function for the perils its conditions name, evaluates over the result together with the source graph, and discards it. A deployment may persist the result as a derived artefact, and nothing requires it to.
