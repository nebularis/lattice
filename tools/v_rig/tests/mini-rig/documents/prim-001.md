# Primary Liability Policy

This document is invented for the repository's tests and binds no one.

## Declarations

| Item | Entry |
|---|---|
| 1. Insured | Invented Holdings Limited |
| 2. Policy Period | 2027-01-01 to 2027-12-31 |
| 3. Limit of Liability | XCD 1,000,000 each occurrence and in the aggregate |
| 4. Retention | XCD 25,000 each occurrence |

## Section 1 Insuring Agreement

1.1 The Insurer will pay on behalf of the Insured those sums the Insured becomes legally obligated to pay as damages because of bodily injury or property damage to which this policy applies, subject to Section 3 and to the Limit of Liability in Item 3.

1.2 This policy applies only to bodily injury or property damage that occurs during the Policy Period stated in Item 2.

## Section 2 Definitions

1. **Insured** means the entity named in Item 1 of the Declarations and any subsidiary it controls.
2. **Occurrence** means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions.
3. **Policy Period** means the period stated in Item 2 of the Declarations.

## Section 3 Exclusions

The Insurer will not pay for damages arising out of any of the following.

1. Liability the Insured assumes under a contract, except liability the Insured would have had in the absence of the contract.
2. Bodily injury to an employee of the Insured arising out of employment.
3. Property damage to property the Insured owns or rents.

## Section 4 Conditions

4.1 The Insured shall give the Insurer notice of an occurrence as soon as practicable.

4.2 Where other valid and collectible insurance applies, this policy is excess of it unless that insurance is written to be excess of this policy.
