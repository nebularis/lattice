# Ontology Releases

Version IRIs do not dereference yet. Until they do, a released version is fetched from
GitHub at its tag, `<name>-vX.Y.Z`, following the
[ontology versioning policy](ontology-versioning-policy.md#releasing-a-version). A layer's
shapes and projections are released on their own, versioned by their directory's `.version`
file, and have no version IRI.
