## 9. Further reading

This page stays deliberately at the level of "why," with just enough of "what" to make the why concrete. For the fuller vocabulary and the mechanics each layer actually declares, see [the LATTICE glossary](./glossary.md). For the worked, running-code version of everything Surface, Eligibility, and MORK do, see [the Field Guide](./book.html). For the record of each individual decision named above, see the [ADR index](./adr/) — in particular ADR-A16 through ADR-A20 for Surface's conservativity laws, and the root `mork/README.md` and `spc/README.md` for the fuller mathematical treatments this page has only summarised.
