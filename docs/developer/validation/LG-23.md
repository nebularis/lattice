# Validation Pack: LG-23, the rounding difference

**Unit:** `ledger` ([plan](../plans/ledger.md), LG-23, [status](../status/ledger.md)).
**Brief:** the ledger plan's "LG-23, the rounding difference", added 2026-11-16 and built after LG-24.
**Decisions:** LG-Q30 (declare the term, each direction stated by the wording), LG-Q33 (the order that places units), [ADR-A131](../../architecture/decisions/ADR-A131-ledger-layer.md)'s second addendum of 2026-11-17.
**Depends on:** LG-24, whose merge policy carries the terms, and LG-20, whose laws LH1 and LH2 it reuses.

## Invariant

Where shares are rounded to the nearest beside a residual allocation, the policy says how the difference is settled, and where it is settled it says where a unit short goes and where a unit over is taken from. Under every pair of those two statements the grants sum to the amount exactly and each stays within one unit of its exact share, wherever the declared order tells the requests apart. No policy is left to a default, and no constraint reads a term that is not declared.

## Deliberate non-coverage

- **No runtime record uses the terms.** The accepted example is configuration only. A merge record that shows a unit taken back waits for the runtime document's rework.
- **The order is assumed injective on the keys.** With no order declared, or one that ties two requests, LH3 says nothing, and the existing rule holds: a unit that needs an order is left where the policy permits a residual, and the merge is refused where it does not.
- **`ldg:ShownOnly` has no law of its own.** It is the residual allocation's rule over shares rounded down, which LR2 and LR3 already cover.
- **No constraint refuses an excess recovery stated beside `ldg:ShownOnly`**, where it has no effect.
