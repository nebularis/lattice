# Validation Pack: AOR-4, consumer catalog and import resolution

**Unit:** [`applied-ontology-readiness`](../status/applied-ontology-readiness.md)
**Plan section:** [AOR-4](../plans/applied-ontology-readiness.md#aor-4-consumer-catalog-and-import-resolution)
**Decision:** [ADR-A88](../../architecture/decisions/ADR-A88-ontology-import-resolution-for-consumers.md), Proposed

## Invariant

Every LATTICE document loads with its import closure from a checkout, with no
machine-specific configuration, and an applied ontology can reach LATTICE by
chaining its own catalog to LATTICE's.

## Artefacts to inspect

- `ontology/catalog-v001.xml`: 55 in-tree entries and 5 external ones.
- The 19 stub catalogs, each a single `nextCatalog`. Six replace the earlier
  hand-made catalogs, which named pre-reset IRIs and absolute paths on one
  machine. The MORK stub replaces a mapping of the Mork IRI to `Mork.owl`. The
  root catalog maps it to `Mork.ttl`, the maintained source.
- `KNOWN_DEFECTS` in `tools/ontology_catalog.py`: seven pre-existing defects
  (status record).
- `docs/architecture/ontology-architecture.md` §2, the consumer paragraph.

## Adversarial probe (run by the agent)

Making `Catalog.lookup` ignore `nextCatalog` failed AOR4-02 and AOR4-11.
Disabling the entry-declares-IRI comparison failed AOR4-04. Restoring the file
returned 11 passes.

## Manual step for the human

Open `ontology/behaviour/spec/behaviour.ttl` in Protégé and confirm the import
closure loads with no missing-import prompt. This settles ADR-A88's open
question on relative `nextCatalog` support. If it fails, the fallback is full
per-directory catalogs, generated by the same tool.

## Deliberate non-coverage

- External locations are listed, not fetched.
- Documents that do not parse are reported, not catalogued.
- Serving documents at their IRIs (ADR-A88 item 5).
