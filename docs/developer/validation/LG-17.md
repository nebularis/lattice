# Validation Pack: Ledger LG-17, what LG-16 left open

**Unit:** `ledger` ([plan](../plans/ledger.md), [status](../status/ledger.md)). **Builds on:** LG-16 ([its pack](LG-16.md)). **Decisions:** LG-Q10, LG-Q9 (a), LG-Q23, ADR-A124, STD-155, STD-176. **Findings:** TSD-71, TSD-72.

## Invariant

On the two-level corpus every read form (the plain fold, the shortcut, a kind-filtered read in the sum with cuts' own `UNION` form, and the sum with cuts) equals the model at every ancestor, read point and mode on every engine, and the sum with cuts equals the stepwise balance. A cut that copies its position gives the same balances as the joined cut. Where a ceiling is above a stated amount, the writer, the model and the stepwise read clamp at the ceiling, and the sum with cuts is still the stepwise balance. The sketch's tree and history, loaded into an engine, read the sketch's figures. This pack holds part one of three. Parts two and three are filled in as they are built.

## One command

```bash
mise run check:ledger-credit-kinds
```

`LEDGER_KINDS_QUICK=1` runs the smallest size, and `LEDGER_KINDS_NEW_ONLY=1` skips the one-level corpus and runs only what LG-17 added. The task runs the tests (82), then the one-level corpus, the two-level corpus with its like-for-like reads, the cut forms and the ceiling variant.

## Deliberate non-coverage

Concurrent writers, the cost of writing and late postings, which are part two. The native Jena caller on the two-level corpus, which is part three. A query plan for any engine, since the Oxigraph Python binding, the Jena test kit and rdflib offer none to read. A ceiling that moves in time. The ceiling variant is read at 30% credits only and without the plain fold or the shortcut. The shortcut's soundness on the two-level corpus is checked by the model comparison of every read and not proved.

## Handoff

### Part one, read forms and storage

Built 2026-11-12 on machine R by a subagent, then checked by the main session, which ran the 82 tests again (82 passed in 158 s, exit 0) and read the report and the change in full.

**What was built.** The two-level corpus gains, under `like_for_like`, the plain fold, the shortcut and `kind-filtered-union`, the kind-filtered sum of offered amounts in the three-branch `UNION` form of the postings part of the sum with cuts. The shortcut's check counts, on this corpus, the credits posted anywhere in the read account's subtree against what each account on the path has outstanding, since a credit at an internal account restores every accepting account above it. The one-level check is unchanged. A cut has a named storage choice, `cut_form`, `joined` (3 triples, the default) or `copied` (4, with `l:cutPosition`), and `run_backend_cut_forms` loads each into its own store and reads the sum with cuts from the start and the checkpoint. An account has a `ceiling_amount` and a `ceiling`, which is the stated amount unless set, the writer, the model, the stepwise read and the shortcut's check use the ceiling, and `l:ceilingAmount` is stored only where set, so every default count is unchanged. `build_ceiling_tree` gives 11 of the 30 contracts and sublimits a ceiling of 1.5 times their stated amount, and `run_backend_cuts(..., ceiling=True)` reads the variant. `using_tree` runs the benchmark's functions on another tree, and `run_sketch` loads the sketch's four-step history into each backend and reads every fold. The environment switch `LEDGER_KINDS_NEW_ONLY=1` is documented in the task's description.

The shortcut falls back in 27 of 27 reads from the start and in 0 (5%) and 13 (30%) of 27 from the checkpoint on every backend. The ceiling variant has 2,190 cuts at 20,000 postings (85 and 550 at 1,000 and 5,000), against 2,160 in the two-level corpus. On the model 2 accounts of 33 stand above their stated amount at some read point at 20,000 postings (7 and 3 at the smaller sizes), which is 4 of the 27 ancestor reads (6 and 5), and the sum with no cuts is wrong at 6 of 9 ancestors. Its read times agree with the two-level corpus's to within run-to-run variation, for example the sum with cuts against the kind-filtered fold at the root is 0.73, 0.57 and 1.17 against 0.68, 0.57 and 1.23.
