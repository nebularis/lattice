# Validation Pack: LG-24, the merge policy node

**Unit:** `ledger` ([plan](../plans/ledger.md), LG-24, [status](../status/ledger.md)).
**Brief:** the ledger plan's "LG-24, the merge policy node", added 2026-11-17.
**Decisions:** LG-Q32 (a policy node, the permission a boolean), LG-Q33 (two order criteria, two guards, no separate rule for a tie), LG-Q35 (one policy, resolved before the ledger), LG-Q36 (versioned and named by version), [ADR-A131](../../architecture/decisions/ADR-A131-ledger-layer.md)'s addendum proposed 2026-11-17, ADR-A142.
**Depends on:** LG-9, whose README it edits.

## Invariant

What an account declares for a merge is stated once, on a merge policy, and every account definition names exactly one. So no account definition states a merge rule, a rounding method or a residual allocation itself, every policy states exactly one merge rule, and each rule the layer checks about those three properties is checked on the policy. A policy is versioned, so the policy a definition names cannot change under it (ADR-A142 decision 1).

## Deliberate non-coverage

- **`ldg:roundingDifference` is still not declared**, so `ldg:NearestRoundingStatesTheDifferenceShape` refuses every policy that rounds to the nearest beside a residual allocation. LG-23 declares it.
- **The definition of `ldg:mergeRule` does not yet speak of ranks.** That ranks are served in turn, with the merge rule deciding among requests of one rank, waits for the rank on a request, a runtime term of the vocabulary's rework.
- **Foundation's shapes are not run on the examples here.** Each policy carries one persistent identity, as `fnd:VersionedShape` asks, and the ledger's tests validate with the ledger's shapes only. The account definition is not yet versioned, so Foundation's check of a meaning-bearing edge does not yet reach `ldg:mergePolicy`.
- **No persistence profile for Ledger exists**, so nothing compiles the edge as pinned yet.
- **The reasons for a refusal** and the kinds of split are not declared here (LG-Q31, LG-Q34).

## Handoff

Built 2026-11-17 on `docs/placement-vehicles-structure`.

**What was built.**

- `ldg:MergePolicy`, a subclass of `fnd:Versioned`, with `ldg:mergeRule` exactly one and each other property at most one. `ldg:mergeRule`, `ldg:roundingMethod` and `ldg:residualAllocation` have it as their domain.
- `ldg:mergePolicy` on the account definition, exactly one, marked `fnd:meaningBearing`.
- New on a policy: `ldg:rankCriterion` and `ldg:unitOrderCriterion`, with the scheme `ldg:OrderCriteria` declared with no member, and the booleans `ldg:mayLeaveResidual`, `ldg:requiresDistinctRanks` and `ldg:refusesMixedPurposes`.
- `ldg:ProportionalNeedsRoundingShape` and `ldg:NearestRoundingStatesTheDifferenceShape` target the policy, and the runtime law that compares a merge's grants with what was available reads the rule through the definition's policy.
- Eleven examples moved, each with one policy and its identity, two refusal examples added, the test-support package `tools/testing/ledger_corpus` generating one shared policy for a corpus, and the tutorial page on drawing down.
- Release notes under Unreleased in the README, and an addendum to ADR-A131 proposed for the maintainer's acceptance.

**Deviations from the plan, each with its reason.**

- The plan said thirteen examples name the moved properties. Eleven do, and thirteen was a miscount.
- The verification rig's mapping scheme was not touched, since it names none of the three properties.
- The release notes also record two changes that LG-9 made and left without a note: the definitions of three concepts that say an order is declared, and the new constraint on rounding to the nearest.
- One sentence of the README said the runtime terms were not yet specified. It was wrong before this slice and is corrected, since the paragraph it sat in was edited.
- The examples and the corpus package were moved by a Sonnet subagent from an exact specification, and checked here by the tests and by reading its report.
