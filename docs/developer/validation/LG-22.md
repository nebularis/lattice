# Validation Pack: Ledger LG-22, rows named by what they hold, request lists that continue the preload, and two probes

**Unit:** `ledger` ([plan](../plans/ledger.md), [status](../status/ledger.md)). **Builds on:** LG-19 ([its pack](LG-19.md)) and LG-14 to LG-18 through it. **Decisions:** LG-Q23, LG-Q26, ADR-A124. **Brief:** [the cloud brief of 2026-11-15](../notes/cloud-brief-lg-22-2026-11-15.md).

## Invariant

A request list for a store preloaded with `n` postings continues the stream the preload came from, so one writer sent it in order against that store refuses no debit for lack of availability, and the lists of several threads differ by seed. A refused request costs the path reads of a write and writes nothing, and a run that did not refuse the requests it was asked to refuse fails. A corpus is named by the share of credits drawn and the share it holds, with its passes, periods, period graphs and postings a period, in every report. A probe is run on demand, fails on a wrong result and never on time, and puts the period length back.

The Jena test kit has to be built first (`mise run bootstrap:sparql-testkit`). The first command is 180 and 22 tests. The first task of the seven takes about two hours on a 4-core cloud machine, the probes 17 minutes and the others between 1 and 14. A comparison of figures needs the tasks run on the code before the change, twice for the variation.

## Deliberate non-coverage

A corpus wide enough to hold a low credit share at 20,000 postings, which is a slice of its own if it is wanted. The space a refusal's own record takes, which the corpus does not model since a refused debit leaves no trace in it. The period length, the tree, the seed, the sizes and the shares, which are the maintainer's to change. The several-writer refusals, which are counted and reported and not suppressed. The reads were not repeated in a pair, so their variation is taken from two runs on two days.

Findings of the work, apart from the figures. The test of `corpus_facts` caught a flag of mine that had the one-level and two-level corpora swapped, and it is fixed. A native test of the writes called `requests` by its old signature and failed until it took the preload. The first refused sample was read after the 500 writes and cost 1.4 to 1.5 times a written debit's reads, which is the reads of a write growing with its tail, so the sample is now read among the writes and the two write tasks were run again.

Not run: the Windows check of the quick tasks, and a pair of runs of the read tasks.
