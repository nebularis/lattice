# Validation Pack: Ledger LG-16, credits at two levels of a path and the cut

**Unit:** `ledger` ([plan](../plans/ledger.md), [status](../status/ledger.md)). **Builds on:** LG-15 ([its pack](LG-15.md)). **Decisions:** LG-Q10, LG-Q9 (a), LG-Q23, ADR-A124, STD-155, STD-176. **Findings:** TSD-71, TSD-72.

## Invariant

Where credits are posted at two levels of one path, an ancestor's balance, read as the stated amount, less the debits in its subtree, plus the credits it accepts at the amount applied, less the cuts recorded at it, equals the stepwise balance at every ancestor and every read point, from the start of history and from a checkpoint, on every engine. The same sum with no cuts is wrong at an ancestor, which is the defect of section 4.8 of the balances sketch.

## Deliberate non-coverage

The native Jena caller on the two-level corpus (`benchmark/ledger-credit-kinds-jvm` is untouched). Concurrent writers, which the cut needs because the writer reads the path balances and records the cut in one step. Late postings. The cost of writing a cut, since cuts are loaded with the history. A 4-triple cut that copies the position. The shortcut and the plain fold on the two-level corpus. A ceiling above a stated amount. Query plans. The sketch's exact tree is tested against the model's functions on a tree built in the test, since the benchmark's tree is fixed, and not loaded into an engine.

## Handoff

Built 2026-11-12 on machine R, and committed after the tests were run again and the report's tables read at review.

**What was built.** A second corpus, `stream_two_level`, with the same draws as the first and `UPPER_SHARE` (0.5) of the credits posted at a sublimit or a section on the drawn contract's path, chosen among the accounts that accept the kind. A sublimit's credit lives in the sublimit's own period graph and a section's in a graph keyed by the section, and an account reads the graphs keyed by every sublimit and section in its subtree. The writer (`settle_credit`) holds every account's stepwise balance, applies a credit at its posting account and records a cut at each accepting ancestor that had less room. A cut is 3 triples in the credit's graph (`l:cutOf`, `l:cutAt`, `l:cutValue`), joined to its posting for the position bound. The model gained the folds `applied` (the sum with no cuts) and `sum-with-cuts` and the list of cuts. The fold `sum-with-cuts` is two aggregates, a three-branch `UNION` over the postings with no `OPTIONAL` inside the graph block (TSD-71) and an aggregate over the cuts, with the times of each part reported.
