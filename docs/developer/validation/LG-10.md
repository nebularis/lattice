# Validation Pack: Ledger LG-10, the residual rule's published definition

**Unit:** `ledger` ([plan](../plans/ledger.md), [status](../status/ledger.md)). **Decisions:** ADR-A131 decision 6. **Reads:** the [sketch on the residual to the first in order](../sketches/ledger-residual-to-first-in-order.md) §3.

## Invariant

The published definition of `ldg:ToFirstInOrder` says what the kernel and the laws' prose say, one unit of the residual at a time to the first proposals in order. The whole residual to one proposal, as the definition read, grants more than a request even where every weight is positive. How the rule treats a key of zero weight is LG-Q15's and is not changed here.
