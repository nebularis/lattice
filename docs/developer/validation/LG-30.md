# Validation Pack: LG-30, the smaller findings of the vocabulary rework

**Unit:** `ledger` ([plan](../plans/ledger.md), LG-30, [status](../status/ledger.md)).
**Placement:** T0, public substrate.
**Decisions:** [ADR-A131](../../architecture/decisions/ADR-A131-ledger-layer.md), and its addendum proposed 2026-11-20 for where a pass's position comes from. No ADR was edited.

**Status:** built 2026-11-23, verified by the maintainer 2026-11-24.

## Invariant

Three positions are natural numbers, a refusal reason is named for its condition, a ledger's first pass records revision 1, a pass's position is stated, a credit ceiling above a stated amount and a discrete unit with no floor are refused at design time, and the examples cite no decision and no delivery identifier.

## What changed

- **Ranges.** `ldg:passPosition`, `ldg:closePosition` and `ldg:microPosition` have the range `xsd:nonNegativeInteger`. Every literal of them in the examples, the tutorial's chapter 10 and `tools/testing/ledger_corpus` is typed `xsd:nonNegativeInteger`. The benchmarks under `benchmark/ledger-credit-kinds`, `benchmark/ledger-posting-cost`, `benchmark/ledger-store-comparison` and `benchmark/ledger-credit-kinds-jvm` write `microPosition` in a namespace of their own (`https://example.org/ledger-kinds#` and `https://example.org/ledger-cost#`), which is not the layer's, so they are unchanged. `benchmark/ledger-supersession-read` already typed the three as natural numbers.
- **The renamed reason.** `ldg:CrossingLimits` is `ldg:CrossingSets` in sections 6.4 and 6.7 and in the release note that lists the reasons. The kernel's name `Crossing` is unchanged, and the prose of section 10 that maps it, which this slice does not touch, still names the old term.
- **The first revision.** The entries for `ldg:Ledger`, `ldg:Pass`, `ldg:revision` and `ldg:passRevision` say an owner with no ledger is read as revision 0, so a ledger's first pass records 1. `ldg:PassRevisionsInOrderShape` also reports a pass whose revision is not 1 where no pass of its ledger records a lower one.
- **A pass's position.** The entries for `ldg:Pass` and `ldg:passPosition`, and a bullet of section 5.1, say it is the later of its stimulus's position and its ledger's latest pass's position at the revision it was evaluated from. No shape changed.
- **Two constraints.** `ldg:CreditCeilingAboveStatedAdmittedShape` reports a definition whose ceiling is above its stated amount unless some `ldg:LedgerProfile` in the data states `ldg:admitsCeilingAboveStated` true. `ldg:DiscreteUnitStatesAFloorShape` reports a definition whose unit of account has `qnt:densityKind` `qnt:Discrete` and no `qnt:granularityFloor`. Each is one node shape on `ldg:AccountDefinition` that reads every definition in one query. The absence of a profile and of a floor is found with `FILTER NOT EXISTS`, as the neighbouring constraints find an absence. The count of constraints in section 5.1 and 7.2 is thirteen, and the two open issues they closed are removed from section 13.
- **Examples.** Two refusals, `refusal-ceiling-above-stated.ttl` and `refusal-discrete-without-floor.ttl`. Every other discrete space states a floor of 1 (13 examples, and the two generated spaces of `ledger_corpus`). No accepted example had a ceiling above its stated amount, so no profile was added. The comments of 13 examples no longer cite a decision, a slice, a question or a debt.
- **Section 8** names the accepted example that holds no records, and the two new refusals.
- **Tests**, in `tools/test_ledger.py` and `benchmark/ledger-supersession-read`, as below. `test_the_corpus_is_versioned_as_foundation_asks` is unchanged. The hard count of examples is 22, and `_floor_dropped_for_integers` also makes the space dense, since a discrete space with no floor is now refused by its own constraint and that case isolates the datatype.

## Probes of the new tests

Each new test of the adversarial list was shown to fail where the code it guards is broken, and the file was then restored.

- **A running balance reads the cuts.** In `shapes/runtime.ttl`, `ldg:RunningBalanceShape`'s `- COALESCE(?cutValue, 0),` became `- 0,`. `test_a_running_balance_reads_the_cuts_at_its_account` failed with `AssertionError: {'...ledger#RunningBalanceShape'}`, on the positive case, which the shape then reported. The file was regenerated from the README and is identical to what it was before the probe.
- **A read at the revision of a supersession.** In `stated_in_force`, the branch for the per-version and per-pass variants had `FILTER(?rev <= {revision})` become `FILTER(?rev < {revision})`. The first run of this probe changed the branch for a record an account by mistake, and the test failed for that variant, with `per-account on oxigraph: R2 of sub0/site0000 as of revision 31 reads 999999129, the model gives 1000999132`. The review repeated it on the branch named here, and `test_ss4_each_variant_reads_the_models_values_at_every_sampled_point[2-per-version]` and `[2-per-pass]` failed with the same two values, so the added points catch the break in either branch. With the earlier read points and the same broken line every test passed. The file was restored each time.
- **The conversion terms are gone.** In `spec/ledger.ttl`, the line `ldg:conversionMethod a owl:ObjectProperty .` was added at the end. `test_the_conversion_terms_are_gone` failed with `AssertionError: ledger.ttl`, naming the term. The file was restored from the index, nothing was left changed, and the test passed. Run by the review on 2026-11-24, since the probe of the build was not written up here.

## Deliberate non-coverage

- Section 10 and `tools/proofs/` are untouched, so the kernel's statement of the first revision and of a pass's position is not formalised, and no proof was run.
- No shape checks a pass's position against the position of its ledger's latest pass.
- The credit-ceiling constraint reads every profile of the data, not the profile of the owner's ledger, since a definition names no ledger.
- No test pins the cost of the two new constraints beyond the suite's time.
