## Invariant

Every merge records one `ldg:Request` for every request made at its account and position, granted or refused. A merge that granted states no reason, gives every request a granted value from zero to what it asked, and its granted values and its residual sum to the lesser of what the requests asked and the availability it was given. A refused merge states one reason of `ldg:RefusalReasons`, grants no request, states no residual, and no posting cites it. A credit refused because its account does not accept its kind is the same record with one request and `ldg:KindNotAccepted`. A residual above zero stands only where the policy allows residuals, and a policy names a residual account only beside that permission. A request states a rank exactly where its policy states a rank criterion, and a place exactly where it states a unit order criterion.

### The seven reasons and the kernel

The kernel's `reason` has six constructors, `Exceeds | Unranked | Tied | Undecided | Mixed | Crossing`, all for a merge. The decision has seven. The seventh, from LG-Q31 and the fourth addendum of 2026-11-16, is a credit posted to an account that does not accept its kind, which the kernel's `post` refuses by returning no posting and stating no reason. The kernel is not changed, since no decision asks that `post` return a reason and LG-Q31 is about the runtime's scheme. The README maps the six constructors to six concepts and says `ldg:KindNotAccepted` is a credit `post` refuses.

### Choices this slice made

- **The names of five reasons.** LG-Q31 asked for names that state the condition and gave two. The others are `ldg:ExceedsAvailable`, `ldg:RanksNotDistinct`, `ldg:MixedPurposes`, `ldg:CrossingLimits` and `ldg:KindNotAccepted`.
- **The rule for requests of no use in part is a value of `ldg:mergeRule`, `ldg:WholeOrNothing`.** The second addendum of 2026-11-16 calls it a rule and the sketch carries it on the merge rules, and the kernel's `whole_each` is a rule over every request at a position. Its name is this slice's.
- **A granted value on every request of a merge that granted, zero included.** The decision says "what it was granted where the merge granted", read as where the merge's outcome is `Granted`. So `ldg:GrantedMergeShape` asks a granted value of every request, and the request of 4 in `whole-or-nothing.ttl` states 0.
- **The sum a merge that granted is held to.** The kernel gives `Granted v 0` where the requests fit, and otherwise grants `g` with the residual `x - total g`, with `x` the availability not below zero. So the granted values and the residual sum to the lesser of what was asked and that availability, under every rule. The law before this slice held only a proportional merge to the whole availability.
- **A residual of zero.** The kernel grants with a residual of zero whether or not the policy allows residuals, so `ldg:ResidualPermittedShape` refuses a residual only above zero, and below zero always.
- **The guards follow the kernel's order.** `merge_of` grants requests that fit before it checks the ranks, so `ldg:MergeGuardsShape` refuses a shared rank only where the requests exceed the availability. `settle_purposes` checks mixed purposes before anything, so it refuses differing purposes on any merge that granted. A request with no purpose agrees only with one with no purpose.
- **A rank only where a criterion is stated.** LG-Q18 has each request carry the rank derived from the policy's criterion. The second addendum of 2026-11-09 has an order declared and never taken from identifiers, so `ldg:RanksAsDeclaredShape` also refuses a rank where the policy states no criterion. The place follows the same rule against `ldg:unitOrderCriterion`.
- **The refusal reasons state their scheme.** A refused merge's reason is checked to be a concept of `ldg:RefusalReasons`, so the scheme's table carries a `skos:inScheme` column. No other scheme of the layer states membership.
- **`ldg:residualAccount` is not marked meaning-bearing.** LG-28 marks the account definition's edges. Whether the policy's edge to the account that takes a residual bears meaning is asked below.
- **The credit refused alone has no availability.** Its record states no `ldg:availableBefore`, so the merge laws that read one pass it by.

## Deliberate non-coverage

- **A merge record with no `ldg:availableBefore`**, as the research grant's are, is not summed against availability, and the guard on distinct ranks is not applied to it. That was so before the slice.
- **The order within a rank is not checked against the places.** No law asks that a unit placed by `ldg:ToFirstInOrder` or taken by `ldg:FromFirstInOrder` went to the place the record states. The grants are checked to sum, and the places are recorded.
- **`ldg:WholeOrNothing`'s grants are not recomputed.** No shape asks that each request fitted or not in rank order, as no shape recomputes a proportional share.
- **A residual is not posted.** No example posts a residual to the residual account, since the decision says only that it may be, and that account's own policy says what becomes of it.
- **The plan's LG-2 vocabulary table** still names `ldg:Grant` and `ldg:grant`. It is a planning record and is not edited.
- **The status records, the board and the plan** are not edited. The coordinator does.
