## Invariant

A posting records the running balance of the account it is on. An account's balance is its stated amount, less the debits in its subtree, plus the credits it accepts at the amount applied, less the cuts recorded at it, and the runtime laws hold every posting's running balance and every checkpoint to that sum. A credit carries a kind and the amount offered beside the amount applied, is posted only to an account that accepts its kind, and is cut at each accepting ancestor that had less room, by exactly the room it lacked. No record holds a balance for each account and pass.

### Choices this slice made

- **The order a running balance reads.** LG-Q9 says the order within a pass is pass position then micro-position, and the authority example has three postings of one pass on one account told apart by recorded position alone. So a posting counts, on its own account, the postings at or before its recorded position and micro-position, and on an account below it, those of an earlier pass or of its own pass at or below its micro-position. `ldg:microPosition`'s definition now says it also orders a pass's postings across accounts.
- **What a credit's applied amount is checked against.** No shape recomputes the room from the balances. `ldg:CreditAmountsShape` asks that a credit applied at less than it offered leaves its account at its ceiling, and `ldg:CutFillsTheAncestorShape` asks that an accepting ancestor is not above its ceiling after the credit, and is at it where a cut is recorded. With the cut positive and at most the amount applied, those two conditions hold exactly where the kernel's `credit` gives the amount and the cut.
- **A credit with no kind.** It is accepted only by accounts that name no kind, and `ldg:CreditKindAcceptedShape` refuses it on any path where an account names its kinds, as the first addendum of 2026-11-16 says. The kernel's `post` refuses only at the account posted to, so the law is stricter than the kernel and agrees with it wherever the law admits a credit.
- **The checkpoint.** It keeps its one balance, now the sum less the cuts. Its two flow totals are LG-27's.

## Deliberate non-coverage

- **Two postings of one pass on different accounts of one path at one micro-position** are each counted in the other's running balance. No law refuses the tie, and none of the examples or corpora holds one.
- **A credit's kind is not checked to be a member of `ldg:CreditKinds`.** No shape reads a scheme's membership, as none does for `ldg:OrderCriteria`.
- **A debit is not checked against availability.** The merge laws check grants against the availability given, and no law here asks that a running balance stay at or above zero.
- **The examples state no checkpoint.** A checkpoint is written at a close, and the closing recurrence moves to the ledger profile in LG-27. The tests add checkpoints to the research grant and the cut example.
- **The debt register is not edited.** TD-158's condition is met: every sum adds zero for a row that is not a counted posting, `DIVERGES_ON_OXIGRAPH` is gone, and LG25-16 holds both engines to one result. TD-159's available shape is gone, and every runtime shape took 1.72 s on Oxigraph at 5,000 postings. Closing them is for the coordinator.
- **The ADR is not edited.** No addendum was needed, since the laws state what the first and fourth addenda of 2026-11-16 already decided.
- **The release register** is not edited, since no version moves on the branch.
