# Validation Pack: LG-29, the supersession and the account's key

**Unit:** `ledger` ([plan](../plans/ledger.md), LG-29, [status](../status/ledger.md)).
**Placement:** T0, public substrate.
**Decisions:** [ADR-A131](../../architecture/decisions/ADR-A131-ledger-layer.md), its addendum proposed 2026-11-23 for an account's coming under a later version of its definition, and [LG-Q57](../sketches/ledger-definition-versions-in-force.md), whose option (a) is built. No ADR was edited.

**Status:** built 2026-11-23, checks as recorded below.

## Invariant

An account is identified by its definition's identity, its owner, its key and its period. A later version of a definition reaches the accounts of one owner by a supersession, a record a pass writes, and two versions of one identity keep the unit of account, the period recurrence, the key dimension, the purposes they count and the parent. A supersession names at most one version of each identity, states a period key exactly where a version it names states a recurrence, and is effective no later than its pass. A pass that writes only a supersession is a pass with a record.

## What changed

- **The record.** `ldg:Supersession`, a `fnd:DerivedArtefact` of the runtime document, disjoint with the other runtime classes. It states `ldg:forOwner`, `ldg:inPass` and `ldg:effectivePosition` exactly once, `ldg:supersedingVersion` at least once, and `ldg:periodKey` and `ldg:refusalReason` at most once. The domains of the five shared properties include it.
- **`ldg:supersedingVersion`.** An object property from `ldg:Supersession` to `ldg:AccountDefinition`, not functional. It is not marked as bearing meaning, as `ldg:ofDefinition` is not.
- **The key.** `ldg:Account`, `ldg:ofDefinition` and `ldg:AccountDefinition` say an account is identified by its definition's identity and names the version in force for its owner at the first position of its period. `ldg:AccountNaturalKeyShape` reports two accounts that share the identity of their definitions, an owner, a key and a period.
- **Admission and overdrawn.** `ldg:admitsStatedBelowDrawn` on an account definition or a ledger profile, and `ldg:overdrawnValue` on a posting, each functional with a cardinality restriction. No shape reads either. The first is not marked as bearing meaning, since the mark makes Foundation's check warn on a literal.
- **The eighth reason.** `ldg:StatedBelowDrawn`, a concept of `ldg:RefusalReasons`.
- **The constraint.** `ldg:VersionsKeepTheAccountsPlaceShape` on `ldg:AccountDefinition`.
- **Three laws.** `ldg:SupersessionNamesOneVersionOfEachDefinitionShape` and `ldg:SupersessionPeriodFromRecurrenceShape`, and `ldg:NotForwardDatedShape` now targets a supersession. `ldg:PassWritesOneLedgerShape` counts a supersession's owner as its records' owners.
- **Two shapes beyond the brief.** `ldg:PassRevisionsInOrderShape` and `ldg:LedgerAtItsLatestPassShape` count a supersession's owner in the same way. Without it the accepted example, whose second pass writes only a supersession, has its ledger at revision 2 while the shape reads the greatest revision as 1.
- **Examples.** `supersession-raised-amount.ttl`, accepted, and `refusal-version-changes-parent.ttl`, refused. The count of examples is 24.
- **Prose.** Sections 4, 5.1, 6.1 to 6.5, 7.2, 7.3, 8, 12 and 13. The class diagram and the axiom index are regenerated by the extractor.

## Probes of the new tests

Each shape was broken in the generated file and the tests that guard it were run, then the file was restored from its backup and the extractor reports it consistent.

- `ldg:AccountNaturalKeyShape`, the join on the identity replaced by the join on the version, so `?definition ^ldg:ofDefinition ?other`. The test of two accounts of two versions failed.
- `ldg:PassWritesOneLedgerShape`, the supersession's owner path removed. The supersession example failed, since its second pass then writes no record.
- `ldg:LedgerAtItsLatestPassShape`, the supersession branch of the union made to match nothing. The supersession example failed.
- `ldg:SupersessionPeriodFromRecurrenceShape`, the branch for a period key with no recurrence made to match nothing. The test of a period key where no version states a recurrence failed.
- `ldg:NotForwardDatedShape`, `ldg:Supersession` removed from its targets. The test of a forward-dated supersession failed.
- `ldg:SupersessionNamesOneVersionOfEachDefinitionShape`, the inequality reversed. Two tests failed.
- `ldg:VersionsKeepTheAccountsPlaceShape`, the parent comparison made to compare the version itself. Seven tests failed, among them the accepted example and the refusal.

## Deliberate non-coverage

- No law of a balance is stated against a later version. `ldg:RunningBalanceShape`, `ldg:CheckpointFoldShape` and the other laws read the stated amount of the version an account names, and the example keeps every running balance one that is right under the first versions.
- The bound of a version that has ended, the refusal of a version below what is drawn, and the account that is over are not stated. No shape reads `ldg:admitsStatedBelowDrawn` or `ldg:overdrawnValue` beyond its cardinality, and `ldg:StatedBelowDrawn` is declared and stated by no record.
- Section 10 and `tools/proofs/` are untouched, so a supersession is not an event of the kernel, no theorem is cited for any new shape, and no proof was run.
- The shapes that walk from an account to its definition and compare or read something versions may differ in are unchanged, and the next slice reads them: `ldg:PartOfAccountFollowsDefinitionShape` compares the parent definition's version, and the shapes of a balance, a checkpoint and a credit read the stated amount, the ceiling and the kinds of the version named.
- LG-Q57 is built on its leaning, option (a), and is not yet answered. A different answer changes one entry and one law.
- No test pins the cost of the new constraint beyond the suite's time.

## What the review changed

- **The account-key law ran twelve times slower on Oxigraph as first built.** On the pass of 5,000 postings the runtime laws took 21 s on Oxigraph, where they took 1.7 s before the slice. Putting each changed law back in turn found `ldg:AccountNaturalKeyShape`. Its join from an account to another account of the same identity sat as two patterns in one `FILTER EXISTS`, which Oxigraph ordered without the binding from outside. Nesting the second lookup in a `FILTER EXISTS` of its own, so each starts from a bound node, brought the laws back to 1.7 s. On pySHACL the same pass takes 38 s, where it took 33 s before the slice, which the new and extended laws account for.
- **The README cites the two addenda as accepted,** since the maintainer accepted both on 2026-11-23 while the slice was building.
- **Two laws were extended beyond the brief, and we keep them.** `ldg:PassRevisionsInOrderShape` and `ldg:LedgerAtItsLatestPassShape` count a supersession's owner as they count a record's, since a pass that writes only a supersession moves its ledger's revision.

## What is left open for the next slices

- **A later version of a period recurrence under one identity.** The constraint admits it, and the supersession's period would then be a bin of a recurrence the accounts' keys were not generated by. Whether a recurrence may have such a version is not decided.
- **`ldg:PartOfAccountFollowsDefinitionShape` and `ldg:PeriodKeyFromRecurrenceShape` compare versions and not identities.** Both hold while an account opens under the version in force at the start of its period, and would not under another answer to that question.
- **A supersession's refusal reason is held only to its cardinality,** and no law reads the admission or what an account is over by.
