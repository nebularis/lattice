# Pending design decisions

**Unit type:** Register, not a unit. It has no status record, no slices and no completion gate.
**Sibling:** the [technical debt register](technical-debt.md).

## Purpose

Design decisions that matter but cannot be made yet: the evidence is missing, a dependency is
undecided, or no unit owns the question. Without a register they sit in a sketch's open questions,
a status line or a chat, and are lost or decided by default.

**What goes in.** A decision, not a defect. The question, why it matters, the options known so far
and what would let it be made.

**What does not.** A shortfall in something that exists, which is technical debt. A question a
current unit already owns, which stays in its sketch or plan. An entry is removed when an ADR,
sketch or plan takes it on, naming that document in the commit.

Each entry says when and where it was raised, what deferring it costs, the options known, and what
it waits for.
